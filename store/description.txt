Save a conversation from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini as Markdown or plain text.

Open the chat you want to keep, click the extension, choose a format and export. The same simple interface works on all three apps. Downloads is the default destination; the native folder picker lets you choose another folder.

Your copy includes the displayed messages, speaker labels, readable formatting, code blocks, tables and available source links. A metadata header records the conversation title, original URL, export date, provider, message count and any marked omissions.

Completed ChatGPT Deep Research reports in the supported embedded layout include their Markdown and citation links. When needed, Chrome asks for optional access to that report's embedded site.

Processing stays on your device. There is no analytics, developer backend, account registration, subscription or conversation upload. The exporter does not read session cookies or provider authentication state or make conversation API requests.

Scope and limits:
- One currently displayed conversation at a time, including ChatGPT guest chats.
- Rendered messages are checked for stability, but server-side history completeness cannot be verified.
- Images, audio, video and attachment files are not downloaded. Known omissions are marked.
- Claude artifacts, Gemini Canvas and other content outside displayed messages are not archived. Gemini Deep Research is not supported.
- Desktop Chrome on macOS has been tested. Windows and Linux are not independently verified yet.
- Keep the window open while reading. Standard downloads are handed to Chrome; check its Downloads list for completion. Selected-folder saves are confirmed after the file stream closes.
