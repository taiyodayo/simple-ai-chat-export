Back to export

A copy, without the fuss.

Save the conversation displayed in your ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini tab. ChatGPT guest chats also work. The page may scroll briefly while messages are checked.

How to export

  1. Open the saved ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini conversation you want to keep. ChatGPT guest chats are included.
  2. Open Simple AI-Chat export for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini from your browser’s Extensions menu. Pin it there if you use it often.
  3. Plain text is the default; Markdown is optional. Save location defaults to Downloads; choose Change to pick another folder. Then select Export conversation.
  4. Find your file in the selected folder. Open it to read or edit your conversation.

Your folder choice lasts until this export window closes. Use Downloads resets it. Chrome shows only the name of a custom folder; default downloads follow Chrome’s download settings. Files saved to custom folders do not appear in Chrome’s Downloads list.

Keep this window open while your chat is read. Downloads are handed to Chrome: check its Downloads list to confirm completion. For a selected folder, keep this window open until Export saved appears. Closing it cancels unfinished work, but cannot undo a file already written.

What’s in the file?

Completed ChatGPT Deep Research reports are included with their Markdown and numbered source links. When needed, choose Allow Deep Research and export and approve Chrome’s request to read the report’s embedded site. Unfinished or unsupported reports stop the export.

The transcript contains the rendered messages in their displayed order. The metadata header holds the title, original conversation link, message identifiers, export date and any omissions. The file is not encrypted; keep it somewhere suitable for its contents.

Text exports cannot include image, audio, video or attachment files. Known omissions are marked. Changing or disappearing messages stop export. Server-side history completeness cannot be verified; this limitation is recorded in the metadata.

Claude artifacts, Gemini Canvas and other content outside messages are not included. Gemini Deep Research is not yet verified.

Markdown viewers may render HTML or load remote images contained in a message. Use plain text to view literal content, or disable remote content and HTML in your Markdown editor. Cloud-synced folders can upload saved files through their own software.

Your privacy

Processing happens on your device. The extension has no analytics, retained conversation cache or developer backend. Your saved files and browser download history remain until you remove them.

The extension reads text from the current page only after you choose Export. It does not read session cookies or authentication state, or make chat API requests. Source links already loaded in messages or supported reports are included. Secrets typed into a message are part of the exported text.

Permissions

Current tab and scripting: checks whether you opened a ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini conversation and reads its displayed messages after you choose Export.

Downloads: Chrome receives a local file through its normal download mechanism. No downloads permission or download-history access is requested. Download started means the file was handed to Chrome; check Downloads to confirm that it finished.

Selected folder: the native picker grants access to the folder you choose. The exporter checks candidate export names and writes the requested file without reading other files or listing the directory. The folder handle is kept only in this window’s memory.

Optional Deep Research access: reads report text and citations already loaded in the chosen chat’s embedded sandbox page. Chrome keeps this site permission until you remove it in extension settings. Cited source pages and research activity are not retrieved.

If something goes wrong

Wait for a reply to finish, then reopen the extension on the conversation you want. An unsupported or incomplete conversation is never quietly exported as complete.

For help, visit the support page or report an issue. Please never send passwords, session tokens or private conversation files.
