A copy, without the fuss.

Save the conversation displayed in your ChatGPT tab. Guest chats need no sign-in. The page may scroll briefly while messages are checked.

How export will work

  1. Open the ChatGPT conversation you want to keep. Guest chats are included; no sign-in is required.
  2. Open Simple ChatGPT Export from your browser’s Extensions menu. Pin it there if you use it often.
  3. Choose Markdown or plain text. Save location defaults to Downloads; choose Change to pick another folder. Then select Export conversation.
  4. Find your file in the selected folder. Open it to read or edit your conversation.

Your folder choice lasts until this export window closes. Use Downloads resets it. Chrome shows only the name of a custom folder; default downloads follow Chrome’s download settings. Files saved to custom folders do not appear in Chrome’s Downloads list.

Keep the export window open until saving finishes. Closing it cancels an unfinished export; check Downloads if a save had already started.

What’s in the file?

Completed Deep Research reports are included with their Markdown and numbered source links. When needed, choose Allow Deep Research and export and approve Chrome’s request to read the report’s embedded site. Unfinished or unsupported reports stop the export.

The transcript contains the rendered messages in their displayed order. The metadata header holds the title, original conversation link, message identifiers, export date and any omissions. The file is not encrypted; keep it somewhere suitable for its contents.

Text exports cannot include image, audio, video or attachment files. Known omissions are marked. Changing or disappearing messages stop export. Server-side history completeness cannot be verified; this limitation is recorded in the metadata.

Your privacy

Processing happens on your device. The extension has no analytics, conversation storage or developer backend. Your saved files and browser download history remain until you remove them.

The extension reads text from the current page only after you choose Export. It does not access authentication tokens or make API requests. Only source links present in the message content are included.

Permissions

Current tab and scripting: checks whether you opened a ChatGPT conversation and reads its displayed messages after you choose Export.

Downloads: saves your export and checks whether that download finished or was interrupted. The browser permission is broader than this use; the extension queries only the download it starts.

Selected folder: the native picker grants access to the folder you choose. The exporter checks candidate export names and writes the requested file without reading other files or listing the directory. The folder handle is kept only in this window’s memory.

Optional Deep Research access: reads report text and citations already loaded in the chosen chat’s embedded sandbox page. Chrome keeps this site permission until you remove it in extension settings. Cited source pages and research activity are not retrieved.

If something goes wrong

Wait for a reply to finish, then reopen the extension on the conversation you want. An unsupported or incomplete conversation is never quietly exported as complete.

Support details will be activated before public release. Please never send passwords, session tokens or private conversation files.
