How export will work

  1. Open the saved ChatGPT conversation you want to keep.
  2. Open Simple ChatGPT Export from your browser’s Extensions menu. Pin it there if you use it often.
  3. Choose Markdown or plain text, then select Export conversation.
  4. Choose where to save the file. Open it to read or edit your conversation.

Keep the export window open until saving finishes. Closing it cancels an unfinished export; check Downloads if a save had already started.

What’s in the file?

The transcript contains messages in the selected branch’s order. The metadata header holds the title, original conversation link, message identifiers, export date and any omissions. The file is not encrypted; keep it somewhere suitable for its contents.

Text exports cannot include image, audio, video or attachment files. Known omissions are marked. If the full branch cannot be verified, export stops.

Your privacy

Processing happens on your device. The extension has no analytics, conversation storage or developer backend. Your saved files and browser download history remain until you remove them.

The current prototype checks the selected tab’s address and can save synthetic test files. It does not retrieve ChatGPT content. A future verified version will retrieve the current conversation from ChatGPT using your existing session.

Permissions

Current tab: checks whether you opened a saved ChatGPT conversation, only after you click the extension.

Downloads: saves your export and checks whether that download finished or was interrupted. The browser permission is broader than this use; the extension queries only the download it starts.

If something goes wrong

Wait for a reply to finish, check that you’re signed in, and reopen the extension on the conversation you want. An unsupported or incomplete conversation is never quietly exported as complete.

Support details will be activated before public release. Please never send passwords, session tokens or private conversation files.
